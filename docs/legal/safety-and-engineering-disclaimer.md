## What LunaiCAD checks — and what it does not

LunaiCAD's automated checks are **geometric**:

- The model is watertight, manifold, and a single fused body (or explicitly
  and intentionally multi-body, e.g. an enclosure base + lid).
- Measured dimensions match the interpreted specification within tolerance.
- Basic 3D-print-readiness heuristics (minimum hole size, wall thickness,
  etc., informed by an optional user-supplied calibration profile — see
  [calibration-profile-disclaimer.md](calibration-profile-disclaimer.md)).

LunaiCAD's checks are **not**, and must never be described as:

- A structural, stress, or fatigue analysis (no FEA is performed).
- A pressure-rating, burst, or leak analysis.
- An electrical-safety certification.
- A fire-code or life-safety certification.
- A medical-device, child-product-safety, or any other regulatory
  certification.
- A guarantee that a physical, printed part will fit or perform as intended
  — dimensional output is a geometric prediction, not a physical
  measurement.

## Approved language vs. banned claims

The product is required to use precise language and avoid overstated claims.
Approved phrasing (used consistently in the UI):

- "Passed LunaiCAD geometric checks"
- "Dimensions match the interpreted specification"
- "Fit guidance is based on the selected calibration profile"
- "Engineering review is required before manufacture"

Phrases the product must never use to describe a design or its checks:
"production-ready" (as a claim about the design, not the tool's template
maturity), "guaranteed fit", "manufacturing certified", "safe for
load-bearing use", "perfect CAD", "accurate from any drawing", "structurally
certified", "safety-certified", "engineer-approved", "ready to manufacture".
The full machine-enforced list (with rationale per phrase) is in
`backend/app/safety/language.py`; `scripts/audit_banned_language.py` greps
the codebase for regressions.

## Engineering review requirement

Every design generated by LunaiCAD — regardless of safety category — is
labeled as requiring review by a qualified professional before manufacture
or use. This is not a category-specific warning; it is the baseline
condition of using an automated CAD generation tool whose checks are
geometric, not engineering, review.

For designs in a high-consequence category (see
[acceptable-use-policy.md](acceptable-use-policy.md)), this requirement is
elevated to a blocking or acknowledgment-gated control, not merely advisory
text — see that document for the category → behavior table.
